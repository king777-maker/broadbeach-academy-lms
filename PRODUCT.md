## Platform

web

## Users
- Quality, EHS, IT, Information Security, and Aerospace professionals.
- Compliance Officers, Internal Auditors, and Lead Auditors preparing for accredited certifications.
- Corporate teams and individual learners pursuing verified ISO standards training.

## Product Purpose
Broadbeach Academy provides globally aligned professional certifications and compliance training in ISO standards (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001, ISO 22000, ISO 19011, Lean Six Sigma). Learners complete structured video and text modules, take rigorous competency assessments, and receive tamper-proof, QR-verifiable certificates.

## Positioning
An accredited professional learning platform combining 100% grant-sponsored foundational certifications and offline lead auditor training with cryptographic instant online verification (ISO 19011 and ISO/IEC 17024 aligned).

## Operating Context
- Self-paced online learning environment with interactive syllabus modules, video lectures, and final examinations.
- Administrative management portal featuring Online Cert Master, Manual Cert Master, Ticket Master, Trash Bin, and Admin Access with MFA and 4-digit PIN verification.
- Public credential verification portal for employers and compliance officers.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single-page application built on React 18, Vite, and Tailwind CSS v4.
- Immutable registered learner names (non-editable through profile/API).
- Persistent support ticket workflow connecting learners with administrative staff.
- Strict security controls: session rate limiting, two-factor TOTP authentication, PIN-gated sensitive certificate operations.
